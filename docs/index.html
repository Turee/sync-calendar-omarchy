Setup guide

Connect your calendar

Open Settings in the calendar panel and click +. A terminal asks which calendar you use and opens the matching section below. Follow the steps, paste what it asks for, and it does the rest.

omarchy plugin add https://github.com/promaaa/sync-calendar-omarchy.git --enable --yes

Google Calendar#

Read & write. Google needs a small app of your own: about 5 minutes, once.

  1. Create a project named Chronica, then make sure it is selected at the top of the page.
  2. Enable the Google Calendar API.
  3. Open Google Auth Platform → Get started. App name Chronica, your email as support and contact email, audience External, then Create.
  4. Audience → Publish app → Confirm. The status should read In production.
  5. Create a client: application type Desktop app, then Create and Download JSON. Keep it in ~/Downloads.
  6. Back in the terminal, press Enter. Sign in in the browser. On "Google hasn't verified this app", click Advanced → Go to Chronica, tick both permissions and Continue.
  7. Pick the calendars to show. Done.

Why publish? An app left in Testing logs you out every 7 days. Publishing your own app needs no review from Google; the "unverified" warning only means the app is yours rather than Google-checked.

Only reading your own calendar? Skip all of this and use its secret iCal link instead (see Any .ics link).

Apple iCloud#

Read & write. Needs two-factor authentication on your Apple Account.

  1. Open your Apple Account → Sign-In and Security → App-Specific Passwords → +.
  2. Name it Chronica and copy the xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx password. Your usual Apple password will not work here.
  3. In the terminal, enter your Apple Account email and that password, then pick your calendars.

Fastmail / JMAP#

Read & write. Also Stalwart, Cyrus and other JMAP servers.

  1. Fastmail: Settings → Privacy & Security → API tokens → New API token, give it Calendars access (read and write to add events), and copy it.
  2. Other servers: use your account's API or bearer token. The session URL is usually https://mail.example.com/.well-known/jmap.
  3. In the terminal, keep the default session URL for Fastmail and paste the token.

Nextcloud / CalDAV#

Read & write. Also ownCloud, Radicale, Baïkal, Zimbra. HTTPS only.

  1. Nextcloud: Personal settings → Security → Devices & sessions → name it Chronica → Create new app password, and copy it.
  2. In the terminal, enter your server address (e.g. cloud.example.com). For a server other than Nextcloud, replace the suggested CalDAV address with its own.
  3. Enter your username and the app password, then pick your calendars.

Any .ics link#

Read only, nothing to sign in to. Copy the link, paste it in the terminal.

  1. Google: Calendar settings → your calendar → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format.
  2. Proton: Proton Calendar → Settings → Calendars → Share → Share outside Proton → Create link (full details).
  3. Outlook / Microsoft 365: Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → Publish → copy the ICS link.
  4. Anything else: look for a "public", "subscribe", "export" or webcal:// link.

Stuck? Open an issue. Your credentials never leave your machine: they live in ~/.config/omarchy/calendars.json and ~/.local/state/omarchy/.
